# Open work — a list you keep, not a queue a pass fills

Work you have not decided against, written by hand. That is all this file is.

Until v1.1 it was an approval surface: `/curator`, `/flywheel` and `/audit`
appended checkbox rows, a script allocated IDs and swept your ticks into the
signal ledger, and an acceptance rate scored the arrangement. The reference
instance retired all of it on 2026-09-06 (ADR 0038): the queue promised
"seconds per row" and produced 38 rows, 19 still open at the end and four past
a week, with no rejection ever recorded. Rows did not get answered; they aged.
A finding said in conversation and acted on or dropped in the same hour beats a
row answered later.

## Rules

- **Nothing writes here automatically.** No pass appends, no script sweeps, no
  ID is allocated.
- A pass reports its findings in its own log and in the conversation. If you
  want one kept, it lands here as a line — by you, or by a session on your say-so.
- Silence is not acceptance and not rejection. A line sitting here means nobody
  has decided; it does not mean it is approved.
- Work that is finished or dropped leaves the file. The log that did it is the
  record.
